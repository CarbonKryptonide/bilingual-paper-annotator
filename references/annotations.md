# Write annotations that show where the evidence ends

## Paragraphs

- Explain the move in the argument. Describe what this passage establishes, what it makes possible later, or what would be missing without it. Give reasoning-heavy passages more space. A simple transition may need only one sentence.
- Use natural attribution when the source of a claim changes. For example, “作者将……解释为……” introduces the authors' interpretation, while “结合前面的对照，我们可以推断……” signals an inference from the reader. Report visible data as observations and say which evidence supports your interpretation. Keep the author's own uncertainty intact.
- When a reader inference matters, explain its conditions, a credible alternative or an unresolved step. Keep hypotheses and plausible mechanisms conditional. Vary phrasing naturally and omit speculative commentary when it adds little.

> 方法段记录了随机布板和每组十二孔，这让未混合与预混两种操作可以用同一指标比较。随机化有助于减弱孔位与处理条件的关联。这里的十二孔都来自同一块板，因此我们可以评估这块板内的变异，但还不能据此判断效果能否跨板重现。

## Figures and tables

- Guide the reader to the comparison that matters. Identify the relevant panel, axes and units, or rows and columns. Explain groups, symbols, sample sizes and uncertainty measures when they affect that comparison and the paper supplies them.
- State the observed result with the appropriate units and precision. Explain which claim the result supports and why. Keep the plotted result, the authors' interpretation and any additional inference distinguishable. If the figure cannot answer a key question, say what measurement or comparison is missing.
- Check the source image alongside its caption and methods. A value, significance test or conclusion without a verified source should remain unreported. Keep interpretation in the note field so the original caption and translation can be read separately.

> 图 1 的横轴区分未混合和预混，纵轴是变异系数（%）。两根柱分别是 8% 和 5%，相差 3 个百分点。图中的数值与结果段一致，给出同一块板内读数波动较小的初步信号。图上没有误差线，方法也只使用了一块板，因此无法从柱高判断差异的精度或跨板稳定性。
